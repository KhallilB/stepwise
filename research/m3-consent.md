## Taking part in the Stepwise study

**Run by:** [owner name], [contact address]

### What this study is

Stepwise is an open-source tool that shows code running one step at a time, drawn as a picture. We want to find out whether it helps people fix bugs in algorithm problems faster than adding `console.log` statements. We're testing the tool, not you. There are no wrong answers, and a bug you don't fix is still useful to us.

### What you'll do

- Join a 45-minute video call from your own laptop or desktop, and share your screen.
- Fix a small planted bug in a short JavaScript solution, once with Stepwise and once with `console.log`, on two different problems.
- Answer a few questions about what helped and what didn't.

### What we record

- **Always:** the study tool logs how long each fix takes, which steps you look at, and each time you run the tests. These logs don't include your name.
- **Only if you agree:** a recording of your screen and voice. Your camera can stay off either way.
- **Notes** the person running the session takes during the call.

### How we keep it

- Recordings, notes and your contact details are stored privately. They're never published or shared outside the study.
- In anything we publish you're a label such as "P3". We only publish counts, timings and anonymous findings, in the study report in Stepwise's public repository.
- Recordings and notes are deleted once the report is written and checked, at most 90 days after the last session.

### Your choices

- Taking part is up to you. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can take part without being recorded.
- Until the report is published, you can ask us to delete everything we have from your session. Email [contact address].

### Risks

Very few. You'll be writing and debugging code while someone watches, which some people find stressful. The session isn't a test of your skill, and you can stop at any time.
