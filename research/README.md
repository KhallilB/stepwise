## Privacy

This repository is public. Nothing here may identify a participant.

- Names, emails, screener answers, booking details, session notes and recordings are kept privately by the study owner and never committed.
- Participants are referred to as P1 to P8 here. The key from those labels to real people is kept privately too.
- Only counts and anonymous findings are published, for example "5 of 8 practice in JavaScript".
